## Data Processing

### What We Do NOT Collect
- User accounts or registration
- IP addresses or tracking
- Personal information
- Marketing or analytics cookies

### Uploaded Files
Your route files (GPX/TCX/FIT) are deleted immediately after processing. The processed files offered for download are deleted within 60 minutes.

### Anonymous Statistics
For service monitoring:
- Timestamps of usage
- Number of API calls
- Aggregated route statistics (distance, elevation)

These statistics are anonymous and cannot identify users or locations.

### Cookies
No cookies are set. The light/dark appearance follows your system setting and is not stored.

## External Services

**Weather Data (Optional):**  
<a href="https://open-meteo.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open-Meteo API</a> (fallback: <a href="https://www.visualcrossing.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Visual Crossing</a>)  
- Receives route coordinates if you use weather feature
- Subject to usage limits

**Route Import (Optional):**  
<a href="https://ridewithgps.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">RideWithGPS API</a>  
- Receives the route/trip ID and privacy code if you enter a RideWithGPS URL

**Map Display:**  
Your browser loads the map images directly from <a href="https://www.openstreetmap.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">OpenStreetMap</a>  
- Receives your IP address and the displayed map area
